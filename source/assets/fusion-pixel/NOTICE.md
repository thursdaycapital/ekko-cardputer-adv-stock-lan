# Ekko Pixel12 Chinese Compatible — font notices

The derivative family **Ekko Pixel12 Chinese Compatible** contains 23,845 unchanged encoded BMP glyphs from **Fusion Pixel Font 2026.09.01**, 12px monospaced `zh_hans`, plus **180 unchanged compatibility glyphs** from the pinned **M5GFX 0.2.33 efontCN_12**. It is a combined derivative, not a pure Fusion Pixel font.
